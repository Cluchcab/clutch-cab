THE CLEAN-CAR EDIT
The starter edit

Three little upgrades.

Practical ideas. No complicated setup.

01

Keep small items together

A seat-gap organizer may help keep everyday essentials from slipping between the seat and center console. Check fit and seat-belt access before use.

02

Tame the cables

Cable clips can help keep charging cords neat. Route cables away from pedals, steering controls, airbags, and moving parts.

03

Reset your console

Give keys, receipts, and loose change a proper home. A quick tidy-up can make your interior feel calmer without buying anything.
